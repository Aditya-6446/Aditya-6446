### Featured work

**[Digital Payments Explorer](https://github.com/Aditya-6446/Ecommerce_Payments_Analysis)**

A collaborative research snapshot of payment-service listings across 100 countries and territories, rebuilt as a reproducible analysis with Python, SQL, Excel, Power BI and an interactive dashboard.

![Digital Payments Explorer preview](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/Aditya-6446/Ecommerce_Payments_Analysis/main/docs/images/dashboard-overview.png)

The project includes 300 listings and a documented cleaning trail. Its charts count *mentions in the source dataset*; they do not claim real-world market share or payment volume. [Explore the method and findings →](https://github.com/Aditya-6446/Ecommerce_Payments_Analysis#data-and-design) · [How to run the dashboard →](https://github.com/Aditya-6446/Ecommerce_Payments_Analysis#run-the-dashboard)

### How I approach analysis

- **Trace the source.** Keep original records and document every meaningful cleaning choice.
- **Define the measure.** Make it clear what a chart counts, and what it cannot prove.
- **Make it usable.** Turn the result into a dashboard, report or workbook others can explore.
